Foundation

Financial years

Open Financial years

Create the year

Open Financial years, add the Australian financial year label and confirm the 1 July to 30 June dates. Use one year for one reporting period.

Use the header picker

The financial year selector in the header is the shared context for the workspace. Change it before reviewing documents, transactions or reports.

Watch readiness

Dashboard readiness signals missing evidence, incomplete records and next actions for the selected year.

Evidence

Documents

Open Documents

Upload from your device

Choose files, a folder or a photo. Select the financial year and document type first so each file starts with the right context.

Import from OneDrive

Connect OneDrive in Settings, then browse folders from the upload screen. Import one file or a folder without losing the original location.

Keep the original

TaxVault stores the original document with its evidence metadata. Do not replace a source file with a cropped image when the full document is available.

Find it later

Documents can be filtered by year, type and review state. Use Search when you remember a filename, supplier or document detail.

Accuracy

Reviews and extraction

Open Reviews
01

Open the review queue

Start with items that need attention. The review page keeps the original document beside the extracted fields.

02

Compare, then correct

Check supplier, issue date, total, tax and category against the original. Save corrections rather than relying on memory.

03

Keep a traceable decision

Reviewed values remain connected to the source document and the history of corrections, which makes the accountant handoff easier to explain.

Records

Income, expenses and assets

Open Income & expenses

Transactions

Record income and expenses, import bank rows when connected, and categorise the items that support the year.

Properties

Keep property details, ownership, loan information, rental evidence and relevant periods together.

Investments

Capture holdings, disposals, income and cost-base information so capital gains records have their supporting context.

Use the Dashboard to see the combined picture. A complete year is not only a folder of receipts; it also explains the money and assets those receipts support.

Read relevant tax updates and keep notes that may affect what you ask your accountant about. News is context, not a lodged return or tax advice.

Accountant Pack

Review the readiness preview, select the financial year and prepare a clearer summary of records, evidence and open items.

Handoff

When the year is ready, send it clearly

Manage Accountants
  1. Resolve the blockersUse Dashboard readiness and Reviews to finish missing or uncertain items for the selected year.
  2. Prepare the packOpen Accountant Pack, check the included records and confirm that the summary matches the evidence.
  3. Invite or select your accountantIn Accountants, invite the professional or manage an existing connection. Share only the access needed for the work.
  4. Send a short noteTell your accountant which year is ready, what is unusual, and which questions still need their judgement.
  5. Keep the source connectedDo not delete originals after sending. TaxVault is designed to keep evidence, corrections and the handoff traceable.
